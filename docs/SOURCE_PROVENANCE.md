# Source provenance

Source identities, DOI/stable locators, and source-file hashes are recorded in `data/provenance/`. The reproduction route uses repository-contained extracted and standardized factual records rather than third-party PDF or workbook bytes.

Scientific verification fixtures are stored in `results/reference/`. Publication-facing machine-readable outputs are stored in `results/publication/` and are rebuilt by the full pipeline. Where source resolution determines terminal status, the corresponding scientific reference is stored under `results/reference/resolved/` and summarized in the integrated evidence matrix.

The provenance records identify source materials but do not redistribute or relicense them. See `THIRD_PARTY_DATA_AND_LICENSES.md` and `LICENSES/CONTENT_LICENSE_SCOPE.md`.
