# D/O taxonomy

D and O are independent. Final D is the deepest uniquely executable public computational checkpoint after documented source resolution, not merely the presence of data. D0 means the supplied public evidence does not determine a complete, defensible, unique terminal chain; D1 is a defensible near-terminal reconstruction; D2 is a complete standardized checkpoint plus operative weights/aggregation inputs; D3 reaches the earliest public numerical inputs required for the native method. O records published terminal-output depth. NP10 is final D0/O3.
