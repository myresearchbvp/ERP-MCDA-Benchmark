ERP-MCDA benchmark: criterion-deletion methods

This deterministic single-criterion deletion analysis uses method-compatible recomputation. It contains 118 original operations and six post hoc NP11 deletions. All eight applicable lineages have evaluable outcomes; 41 specification rows are NE.

Total executed deletion-outcome rows=124
NP01: 12; NP02: 5; NP03: 30 (15 criteria in each of two branches); NP04: 12; NP05: 30 (five criteria in each of six configurations); NP07: 11; NP11: 6; NP12: 18.

NP01: Remove the subcriterion from its local triangular-fuzzy reciprocal matrix and recompute that fuzzy-AHP weight vector by the geometric-mean/defuzzification rule. Keep the three main-criterion weights fixed, regenerate global weights and recompute TOPSIS.
NP02: Recompute the reduced criteria AHP matrix by column normalization and row means, then combine the retained local alternative priorities.
NP03: Remove the criterion from the normalized D2 checkpoint, renormalize the remaining weights and recompute TOPSIS. Evaluate the same 15 deletions separately for A_STRICT_EXPLICIT_DIRECTION and ERP04_COST_PARALLEL.
NP04: Remove the criterion from the rough-normalized checkpoint derived from the raw source supplement. Keep the remaining printed rough weights fixed and recompute the native rough-WISP K/P aggregates and four utility families.
NP05: Evaluate C1–C5 for all six CE-I/CE-II × Hamming/Euclidean/Hausdorff configurations. Recompute entropy-distance normalization and the p-HFC/COPRAS chain; q=2 and beta=3 are fixed. C6 is NE because removing the sole cost criterion leaves the native cost-side formula undefined.
NP06: NE: the source does not define a reduced Choquet capacity. The reported interaction-off comparison is addressed separately under source-defined sensitivity.
NP07: For the Kılıç et al. (2015) baseline, remove the criterion, renormalize the retained printed ANP weights and recompute PROMETHEE II with V-shape p=2. The linked Temur–Bolat CBDO implementation cannot be reconstructed from the available source material.
NP08: NE: the source does not identify a unique terminal LP solution or tie-break rule.
NP09: NE: the literal computational chain becomes nonfinite before producing a terminal result.
NP10: NE at D0/O3: the operative Qb/Qn mapping and Supplementary Tables S1–S6 are unavailable.
NP11: Recompute criterion weights with LFPP and M=1000 while holding local ERP weights fixed. All six deletions have certified rankings despite nonunique optimal weights and scores. Winner retention is 5/6; complete-ranking retention is 3/6; C6 changes S4 to S3. SLSQP does not converge for C6; independent convex calculations and rational/interval certificates establish its ranking. DEMATEL is separate from ERP ranking.
NP12: Renormalize the retained published weights, hold q/v=0.5 fixed and recompute S, R, Q and the native VIKOR compromise conditions. The recommendation set is the primary outcome; the top-Q leader is a secondary diagnostic.

Retention shares describe the finite set of eligible deletions. They do not estimate real-world choice probabilities or literature-wide robustness rates. Files under historical/ preserve the original execution records; their NP11 NE labels denote nonexecution.
